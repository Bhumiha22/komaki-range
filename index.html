Important limitations

This app cannot read PackDial's private data. It discovers BLE services and characteristics, but cannot safely infer battery percentage from unknown raw bytes. Do not write to any BMS characteristic or change protection settings.

Web Bluetooth requires Chrome/Edge on Android and a secure HTTPS website (or localhost). The app interface can be cached for offline use after the first visit; Bluetooth connection itself works locally without internet.
